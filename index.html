What this proves

Guests get an answer in seconds at any hour, booking details are collected in a consistent format, and staff only spend time on requests worth confirming. In a pilot we would measure after-hours leads captured, reply time, and how many risky requests were stopped early.

Safeguards built in

  • An 18+ age gate before any conversation starts.
  • The concierge never discusses explicit content and never confirms a booking itself; a person always does.
  • Anything suggesting a minor or someone being pressured is blocked and flagged for immediate review.
  • Abusive, haggling or unverifiable requests are flagged before anyone is put at risk.
  • Guest details are kept to the minimum needed, ready for UK GDPR controls.
